## HIG audit of the session-memory surfaces

Guidelines read live from developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines on 2026-09-15 (page JSON; change logs through Layout 2026-09-09, Menus 2026-06-08, Buttons, Color, Typography, and Writing 2025-12-16, Materials and Motion 2025-09-09, VoiceOver 2025-03-07, Alerts 2024-02-02): Menus, The menu bar, Settings, Layout, Typography, Color, Dark Mode, Materials, SF Symbols, Buttons, Pickers, Segmented controls, Steppers, Text fields, Labels, Lists and tables, Alerts, Feedback, Progress indicators, Writing, Accessibility, VoiceOver, Motion. Scope: every surface `1e5e16e` added or reworded under `Sources/Mentor`. F = fixed, C = conforms, L = left, with the guideline in brackets.

**Menu goal line.** C a dimmed status row in the status group, "Goal:" like "Context:" and "Talk back:" [Menus: organization; Writing: language patterns]. F "Goal: still working it out" claimed work in progress while paused, idle, or capped: now "Goal: not worked out yet", parallel to "Context: not judged yet" [Writing: be clear]. C the goal clipped at 64 characters on a word boundary, whole in the debug panel and Settings [Menus: succinct labels, menu length]. C sentence case, like every status row [Writing: one capitalization per element type].

**Understanding card.** F hand-built "Revision N" and "None" capsules (PR 9 review lead, confirmed): Revision is a `StatusBadge`; the "None" capsule is gone because the empty-state sentence already says it [Color: not color alone; Accessibility: Increase Contrast; Writing: every word needed]. F header middle dot in tertiary and read aloud: secondary and hidden, the header one VoiceOver stop, as in the Suggestions rows [VoiceOver: exclude decoration; Accessibility: contrast]. F empty state "Nothing yet. The first mentor call writes one, or the periodic refresh does when no mentor call has.": "No understanding yet. The next mentor call writes the first one, or a refresh call does after 15m of active use without one." [Writing: blank screens]. F evidence indented by a 19 pt constant sat 1 pt off its goal, list headings started at three x positions set by each symbol's width, and wrapped items ran back under their hyphen: one 16 pt column for symbols and bullets [Layout: align, indent for hierarchy]. F hyphen bullets read as "dash": a bullet hidden from VoiceOver [VoiceOver: decoration]. F each goal was four VoiceOver stops and "84%" said nothing about what it measured: one stop reading "84% confidence", with a Confidence tooltip [VoiceOver: grouping; Buttons: tooltips]. F section titles were plain text: headings [VoiceOver: headings]. F "Timeline" named a card section and the panel's Timeline page: "Done so far", beside "Said so far" [Writing: consistent terms]. F "Open concerns" used `eye.trianglebadge.exclamationmark`, the menu bar's waiting-for-permissions symbol: `flag` [Menus: icons represent one thing consistently; SF Symbols]. F the refresh line packed seven facts, said when the next refresh was twice (the second time as "not due, next at 5:24:01 PM" on the locale clock beside "since 15:13:50"), printed "1200" beside "3,390", and gave a cost with no subject: Refresh, Next, Size, Cost ("in refresh calls"), and Last refresh fields, the not-due hold dropped as a repeat, counts grouped [Writing: be clear; Layout: group related information]. F "next in 851s": "in 14m 11s" (`Formatting.countdown` now uses the tested `ClockInterval.description`, so the status bar and Mentor loop card read the same) [Writing: be clear]. F "Last call" meant only refresh calls: "Last refresh" [Writing]. F a refresh in flight showed only as "held: a call is in flight": a spinner row "Refresh call in flight", like the Mentor loop card, and "Next: refreshing now" (`MentorStatus.refreshStanding` gains `.refreshing`, tested) [Feedback; Progress indicators: spinner for background work]. F Reset Understanding forgot every revision with no confirmation: "Reset the understanding?" with a plain Reset Understanding and Cancel, the button titled with an ellipsis [Alerts: confirm uncommon destructive actions that cannot be undone, destructive style only for unintended ones, always Cancel; Buttons: ellipsis]. F Reset Understanding's accessible name (PR 9 review lead): SwiftUI exposes it as AXDescription "Reset Understanding…", which VoiceOver reads; AppleScript's `name` reads the empty AXTitle, which is true of every SwiftUI button in the app, so the drive saw no name. The button now lives outside the per-second timeline redraw as well [Accessibility: label every control]. F Field label and value were separate stops: one ("Next, not counting: excluded app"), in the Mentor loop card too [VoiceOver: grouping]. C a failed refresh shows outcome, model, and the API error in Last refresh, as the Mentor loop card's Last triage and Last mentor do [Feedback: say why]. C text styles no smaller than caption, label colors, text selection on goals and entries [Typography; Labels]. C one-second text ticks only [Motion].

**Settings > Models > Understanding.** C the Understanding model and effort pickers and the section footer on the models [Pickers; Segmented controls]. F "Currently" with a lower-case summary ("revision 7, 320 tokens, $0.041 so far", "nothing worked out yet"): "Current goal", "Revision 7", "320 tokens, $0.041 in refresh calls", "Not worked out yet" [Writing: clear labels, sentence case]. F on screen a real 82-character goal pushed the value onto a ragged trailing line under its label: the goal is the row's wrapping subtitle and the row is one VoiceOver stop [Layout: align for reading; VoiceOver: grouping]. F "Refresh at most every 900 seconds", stepping by 300 up to 43,200: minutes and hours, like "Forget after no activity for" [Writing: plain language; Text fields]. F adjacent duration rows put their fields and steppers at different x positions because the unit pop-up sized to its word: a fixed 84 pt pop-up, which also lines up the Journal pane's retention rows [Layout: align]. F help text "on the way past, for free... before a call of its own is made" and "oldest timeline entries first, so it can never grow without bound": plain, active, and accurate [Writing]. F new-day expiry only in the footer: said under Forget after no activity for [Writing: explain beside the setting]. F footer "cleared by Clear Journal" named a command in another pane and "the last ten minutes alone" ignored the Look back over setting: a link to Journal settings and "recent screens" [Writing: link, not location]. F Reset Understanding sat leading in a column of trailing controls: trailing, like Restore Default Timing [Layout: align]. F no confirmation: the same dialog as the card [Alerts].

F understanding calls and events were teal, the color of Follow-up calls, Replay tags, and talk-back events: brown in the call log, timeline, and card [Color: one color, one meaning]. F OCR confidence column in tertiary (PR 9 review lead, confirmed): secondary [Accessibility: contrast; README Design conventions].
