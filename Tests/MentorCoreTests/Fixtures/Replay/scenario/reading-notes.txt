Reading notes: The Voyage of the Beagle, chapter one

Darwin opens at St. Jago in the Cape Verde islands, in January 1832.
He describes the volcanic rock, the dry plains, and a band of white
shells and coral raised well above the sea, which he takes as evidence
that the whole coast has slowly risen.

Things to look up later:
